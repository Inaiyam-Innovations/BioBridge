EVENT OVERVIEW

BioBridge Startup Summit 2026

Bridging Biology, Research & Entrepreneurship

National Summit · Oct 30, 2026 KSRCT Campus

BioBridge Startup Summit 2026 is a one-day summit bringing together Biotechnology, HealthTech, and Life Sciences startups, researchers, incubation centres, investors, and industry leaders on a common platform.

Biotech & HealthTech Incubators & Labs VCs & Angel Investors
ECOSYSTEM CONVERGENCE

Translation: Scientific Research to Market

The summit aims to strengthen connections across the bio-innovation ecosystem by creating opportunities for incubation support, industry collaboration, entrepreneurial learning, and investor engagement. Through expert interactions, startup experiences, and discussions on investment perspectives, the event encourages innovators to explore pathways from scientific research to market-ready solutions.

Incubation Support Industry Collaboration Investor Perspectives
COLLABORATIVE HORIZONS

Collaboration & Sustainable Growth

With a focus on collaboration and sustainable growth, BioBridge Startup Summit 2026 provides a space for emerging bio-ventures and ecosystem enablers to exchange ideas, discover opportunities, and build meaningful connections.

CORE PROGRAMME

Summit Sessions

The summit is designed around three core sessions, connecting bio-startups with support systems, entrepreneurial experiences, and investment perspectives.

SESSION 01 · MORNING

Bio Enablers Pitch

Incubation Reverse Pitch

Incubation centres and bio-innovation enablers present their programmes, laboratory facilities, mentorship, funding support, and opportunities available to bio-startups.

"Discover support. Find the right incubation pathway."

SESSION 02 · AFTERNOON

Inspiring Bio Startups

Startup Talks

Selected founders share their entrepreneurial journeys, research-to-market experiences, challenges, milestones, and lessons learned while building bio-based ventures.

"Learn from founders building the future of bio-innovation."

SESSION 03 · FINAL 1-HOUR

Investor Needs

Investor Panel Discussion

Investors and relevant experts discuss funding expectations, investment readiness, commercialization challenges, and what investors look for in biotechnology and life-sciences startups.

"Understand the investor perspective and prepare for growth."

Programme Note: These are the three core sessions. Precise start and end times will remain unpublished until the formal programme schedule is fully confirmed.

PARALLEL BIOBRIDGE PROGRAMME
OCTOBER 30 · KSRCT CAMPUS

BioBridge Student Hackathon — Live Grand Finale

The BioBridge Student Hackathon provides a complementary platform for student innovators to develop and showcase solutions to biotechnology-related challenges.

The hackathon and BioBridge Startup Summit share a broader focus on bio-innovation, but each has its own programme and participation process. The summit focuses on startup ecosystem connections, incubation support, founder experiences, and investor perspectives; the hackathon focuses on student-led innovation and solution development.
